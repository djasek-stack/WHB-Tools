Free tools for home buyers

Start here: pick a tool below. Each one is free — a short form (first name and email) unlocks both tools on this device. No lender or agent will call you unless you specifically ask us to share your information.

Know Your Numbers

The form asks: your monthly pay, your monthly debts, how much you have saved for a down payment, and what you pay in rent now — plus a few optional details if you want to fine-tune.

You get: the home price that actually fits your life (not just what a lender would approve), your full monthly payment broken down, the cash you'll need at closing, and how buying compares with renting over the years you'll stay.

Deal Analyzer

The form asks: the address of a home you're looking at, then your income, debts, and down payment.

You get: a plain-English verdict on that home — is the price in line with the market, does the payment fit your budget, and is there room to negotiate — backed by the property record and comparable sales.
